About us

ABOUT STREET VYBZ ENT.
& EVENTS.

Street Vybz Entertainment & Events is a Kenyan event management and production company specialising in the planning, coordination, and execution of corporate, institutional, and social events.

We provide end-to-end event solutions including professional sound systems, LED screens, stage and lighting production, tenting, seating arrangements, décor, and DJ services. Our team is committed to delivering seamless event experiences through quality equipment, technical expertise, and exceptional customer service.

Whether you're organising a conference, product launch, AGM, exhibition, team-building event, awards gala, or outdoor activation, we have the equipment and expertise to bring your vision to life.

Our Mission
To deliver world-class event production solutions that exceed client expectations through professionalism, quality equipment, and dedicated service.
Who We Serve
Corporates, NGOs, government agencies, universities, banks, private companies, and individuals across Kenya.
Our Coverage
Based in Nairobi with the capability to support events across Kenya, from small executive meetings to large-scale outdoor productions.
